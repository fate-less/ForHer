A little letter for you

There's something I've been wanting to tell you...

For You ❤️

Hey, you.

For the girl who became someone special to me ❤️ Hey, you. I don't really know where to begin, because there's so much I'd like to tell you, and somehow, putting it into words still doesn't feel like enough. If you had told me that things would turn out this way, I don't know what I would've said. I remember when I finally told you how I felt, and how I had to sit with all those thoughts afterward. I didn't know exactly what would happen between us, but I knew that what I felt for you was real. And now, somehow, you're here. You're my girl. I don't think you realise how much the little things mean to me. The conversations, the laughter, the moments when we forget about everything else for a while. They're the kinds of things I find myself thinking about long after they've happened. I love seeing you happy. Not because I expect anything from you, and not because you have to be happy all the time, but because your happiness genuinely matters to me. I want to be someone you can laugh with, talk to, trust, and simply be yourself around. I don't want us to rush through what we have. I want to learn more about you, discover the little things that make you who you are, make memories with you, and keep finding new reasons to appreciate you. I want to understand you better with time, not pretend I already know everything about you. And since words alone never seem to be enough, I wanted to make you something. Something that you could open and know that, somewhere between choosing the colours and writing these words, I was thinking about you. So here's a little piece of my world, made for you. I can't promise that every day will be perfect. But I can promise that I'll keep trying to be someone who treats you with care, listens to you, respects you, and never takes the fact that you're in my life for granted. And if I ever struggle to find the right words to tell you how I feel, I hope you'll remember the simplest truth behind all of them: I'm really, really glad it's you. You once became the light in a line I was trying to write. Somewhere along the way, you became someone I wanted to write an entire letter for, too. And this is that letter. With all my affection, Your hubby. ❤️

I'm really glad you're in my life.

Yours, always. ❤️
